# field

* A **field** is defined to be the combination of the following:
  - a set $\mathbb{F}$
  - operation $+: \mathbb{F} \times \mathbb{F} \to \mathbb{F}$ and $\times: \mathbb{F} \times \mathbb{F} \to \mathbb{F}$
    - These operations satisfy the following axioms:
      - commutativity of $+$
      - associativity of $+$
      - existence of **additive identity** $0$
      - existence of **additive inverse** for all elements in $\mathbb{F}$
      - commutativity of $\times$
      - associativity of $\times$
      - existence of **multiplicative identity** $1$
        - $0 \neq 1$
      - existence of **multiplicative inverse** for all elements in $\mathbb{F}$ other than $0$
      - distributivity of $\times$ over $+$
