# function surjectivity

* A [[construct-function|function]] $f:X\to Y$ is **surjective** if every $y\in Y$ is the image of at least one input:
  - For every $y\in Y$, there exists $x\in X$ such that $f(x)=y$.

  * INTERPRETATION:
    * The image of $f$ is the entire chosen codomain $Y$.
