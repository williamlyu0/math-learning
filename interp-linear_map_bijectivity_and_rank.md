# INTERP: Linear map bijectivity and rank

* For a [[construct-linear_map|linear map]] $f:V\to W$ between finitely generated vector spaces, [[construct-function_bijectivity|bijectivity]] is equivalent to:
  - $\operatorname{rank}(f)=\dim V=\dim W$.

  * EXPANSION:
    * Bijectivity means both injectivity and surjectivity. The [[interp-linear_map_injectivity_and_rank|injectivity characterization]] gives $\operatorname{rank}(f)=\dim V$; the [[interp-linear_map_surjectivity_and_rank|surjectivity characterization]] gives $\operatorname{rank}(f)=\dim W$.

    * Conversely, those two equalities imply injectivity and surjectivity respectively. Together they imply bijectivity.

  * INTERPRETATION:
    * [[construct-rank|Rank]] is the dimension of the image. Both equalities are used in this characterization.
