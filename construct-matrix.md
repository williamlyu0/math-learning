# matrix

* A **matrix** over a [[construct-field|field]] $\mathbb F$ is a rectangular array of elements of $\mathbb F$.
  An $m\times n$ matrix has $m$ rows and $n$ columns.

  * INTERPRETATION:
    * What the entries represent depends on the chosen context. For example, a matrix may record coefficients of equations or coordinates of images under a linear map.
