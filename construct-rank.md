# rank

* The **rank** of a [[construct-linear_map|linear map]] $f:V\to W$ is the dimension of its image:
  - $\operatorname{rank}(f)=\dim(\operatorname{im}f)$.

  * EXPANSION: the dimension of its image
    * If $V$ has a finite [[construct-vector_space_basis_and_dimension|basis]] $v_1,\ldots,v_m$, linearity shows that $\operatorname{im}f$ consists exactly of the linear combinations of $f(v_1),\ldots,f(v_m)$.
      Hence the image is a subspace with a finite spanning collection, which can be reduced to a basis.

  * INTERPRETATION:
    * The image is a subspace even without finite-dimensional assumptions. The finite-dimensional setting above ensures that its dimension is a finite number.
