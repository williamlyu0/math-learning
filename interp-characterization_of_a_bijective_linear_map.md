# INTERP: characterization of a bijective linear map

* A [[construct-linear_map|linear map]] $f:V\to W$ between finitely generated vector spaces is [[construct-function_bijectivity|bijective]] if and only if
  - $\operatorname{rank}(f)=\dim V=\dim W$.

  * EXPANSION:
    * Bijectivity means both [[interp-function_bijectivity_and_injectivity_and_surjectivity|injectivity and surjectivity]]. The [[interp-characterization_of_an_injective_linear_map|injectivity characterization]] gives $\operatorname{rank}(f)=\dim V$; the [[interp-characterization_of_a_surjective_linear_map|surjectivity characterization]] gives $\operatorname{rank}(f)=\dim W$.

    * Conversely, those two equalities imply injectivity and surjectivity respectively. Together they imply bijectivity.

  * INTERPRETATION:
    * [[construct-rank|Rank]] is the dimension of the image. Both equalities are used in this characterization.
