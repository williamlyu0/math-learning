# function

* A **function** $f:X\to Y$ assigns exactly one element of $Y$ to each element of $X$.
  - $X$ is the **domain**, and $Y$ is the **codomain**.
  - The **image** of $f$ is $\{f(x):x\in X\}$.

  * INTERPRETATION:
    * Different inputs may have the same output, and the image may be smaller than the codomain.
